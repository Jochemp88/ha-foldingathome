# Sample data

`lufahwatch3.jsonl` and `lufahwatch3final.json` are recorded Folding@home v8
client traffic, taken from the sample data of the
[lufah](https://github.com/kbernhagen/lufah) project and used under its MIT
license (see `LICENSE-lufah`). The donor name, team number, machine name and
hostname have been replaced with placeholder values.
